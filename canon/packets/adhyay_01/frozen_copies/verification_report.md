## Surprises (the print against the existing files)

1. **adhyay_01_analysis.md line 59** says "Ovi 139 cites the sage Vyas (ब्रह्मे व्यास साचार)". The print reads **ब्रह्मे व्याप्त साचार**, so Vyas is not in the text. This needs correcting.
2. **adhyay_01_analysis.md lines 81 and 209, and user_answers A1-11** put "मूर्ति अवघी दिगंबर" at ovi 91. The print has it at **ovi 92**. The शीत line is **ovi 94**, not 93.
3. **adhyay_01_analysis.md line 77** spells the word "भ्रूकुटी". The print has **भृकुटी** (the meaning is unchanged).
4. **user_answers_by_id.md line 119** says "पिंगूळ = spotted owlet, a bird, not a plant". The print's context argues against this. In ovi 111 पिंगूळ is paired with मोगरा in a run of plant pairs (ऊस/निवडुंग), and the closing image in 145 is a list of plants. The earlier plant reading (line 65) fits the print better.
5. **user_answers_by_id.md line 127** says ovi 79's जगद्गुरू is Shankaracharya. The print does not name him; that reading is outside knowledge.
6. Adhyay 1 ovi 80 has **गोरख** (not गोरक्ष) and **no मच्छिंद्र**.
7. Adhyay 1 numbering: after ।।१००।। the print also drops the hundreds digit in Adhyay 1, not only in Adhyays 2-4.
8. In Adhyay 3 ovi 128 the visitors flatter Vithoba as "मुख्य धोरणी **महाधूर्त**". The same word is used of the gosavi in ovi 34. This bears on any reading of "महाधूर्त" as praise or blame.
9. The Chandu name is **चंदुमुकीन / मुकीनचंदू** (76, 84). It is neither "Chandu Mukin" as two words nor "Mukundchand".
10. "मी समर्थ कृपेचें पोसणें" (117) and "मी कल्याण समर्थांचा" (118) are **both** in the print, so the flagged conflict in user_answers line 129 dissolves. The "मौंज" claim on the same line is false: the print has ऋतुशांति only.
