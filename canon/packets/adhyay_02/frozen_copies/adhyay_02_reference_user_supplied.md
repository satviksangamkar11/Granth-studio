## Scenes claimed

1. **Bankatlal's anxious search.** After Gajanan vanishes at the end of Adhyay 1, Bankatlal cannot eat or sleep and roams the outskirts and fields of Shegaon day and night looking for him.
2. **Rediscovery in a millet/corn field (Kangaar).** The field belongs to a farmer named "Zama Relee". Gajanan sits among the crops, recognises Bankatlal's devotion and welcomes him; he signals the wandering can stop.
3. **Miracle of the dry tumba.** In midday heat Gajanan says he is thirsty. The nearby stream (odha) is dry or muddy. Gajanan hands over his dry empty tumba (gourd) and tells Bankatlal to dip it in the muddy creek bed; it fills with clear, sweet, cool water.
4. **The name "Gajanan".** Bankatlal asks him to stay at his house in Shegaon. The author says that because he kept muttering "Gan Gan Ganaat Bote", people began calling him Gajanan.
