A 1950s vintage Indian devotional book illustration in matte opaque gouache and egg tempera on aged ivory paper, with soft sepia contours, flat hand-brushed colour and warm mineral pigments of raw sienna, burnt umber, ochre, terracotta and muted sage green.
In the painting, two village men in turbans sit on a stone bench beside the doorway of a mud house with a tiled roof, beside a dusty village road. They turn their heads slightly and talk quietly; one gestures gently with his hand. A few birds glide slowly across the pale sky and leaves move softly in the breeze. The camera slowly and smoothly pushes in a little toward the two men.
