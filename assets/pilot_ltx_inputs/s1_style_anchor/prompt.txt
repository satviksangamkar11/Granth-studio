A 1950s vintage Indian devotional book illustration in matte opaque gouache and egg tempera on aged ivory paper, with soft sepia contours, flat hand-brushed colour and warm mineral pigments of raw sienna, burnt umber, ochre, terracotta and muted sage green.
In the painting, a man sits cross-legged on a dusty village road beside a low wall, with used leaf plates on the ground beside him and a round clay vessel. He slowly lifts a few grains of rice from the leaf plates toward his mouth with calm, unhurried movement and breathes gently, his calm face turned toward the viewer. A faint breeze stirs the dust and the distant bushes. The camera slowly and smoothly pushes in a little toward him.
