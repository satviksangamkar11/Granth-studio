A 1950s vintage Indian devotional book illustration in matte opaque gouache and egg tempera on aged ivory paper, with soft sepia contours, flat hand-brushed colour and warm mineral pigments of raw sienna, burnt umber, ochre, terracotta and muted sage green.
In the painting, two men in turbans sit facing each other on the ground beside a mud wall, with plates and leaf plates of food between them. The man on the right slowly takes a little food from the plate and eats calmly while the man on the left watches with respect and gestures gently. Their shoulder cloths move slightly in a soft breeze. The camera slowly and smoothly pushes in a little toward the two men.
