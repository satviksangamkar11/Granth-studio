## What this tells the director
- Most Adhyays have **2–5 stories**; 1 and 2 are a single continuous story; 19 combines many stories, the long margas teaching and the samadhi; 20–21 are post-samadhi miracles and a closing recap. This matches the owner's rule: structure varies per Adhyay and must not be templated.
- Stories continue across Adhyays (1→2 Bankatlal; Bhaskar Patil 5→11; Bhau Kavar 16→18→20; Pundalik 13→16→18).
- Recurring cast to design once: Maharaj, Bankatlal, Damodarpant, Bhaskar Patil, Balbhau, Pitambar, Hari Patil, Khandu Patil, Krishnaji/Ramchandra Patil, Pundalik, Bhau Kavar, Govindbua; places: Shegaon street, Bankatlal's house, Mahadev temple, Patil's garden, the math, Balapur, Pandharpur, Adgaon well.
