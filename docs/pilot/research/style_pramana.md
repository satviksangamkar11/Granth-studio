### 1.2 Materials and textures (OBSERVED)
- Everything reads as layered cut card/paper: each shape has a slightly lighter inner top-edge (bevel highlight) and soft contact shadow underneath. Edges are crisp, no outlines in dark ink.
- Visible fine paper/linen grain and mottling on large flat areas (tree canopy, night sky, wave rows have faint scratchy fibre texture; most visible in scene A waves and scene C canopy). Estimated grain contrast 4-8%.
- Subtle emboss on shapes: tree canopy lobes show 3 stacked tiers with raised look; waves show a raised lip.
- Details are drawn by thin cut lines and tiny dots: wave "stitches" (dashed white line), saddle dots, beads, flame/spear teardrops.
- Gradient used only softly (haze, vignette), not on character skin; characters are flat fill + subtle inner shading.

### 1.3 Line treatment
- Almost no black outlines. Separation comes from (a) edge highlight, (b) drop shadow, (c) occasional thin light line (hill rims, arches, stitch dashes, wavy grass line) in a lighter value of the layer colour at 20-50% opacity.
- Dashed "stitch" lines on waves: dash ~28 px, gap ~16 px at 923 px width, ~3 px thick.
- Wavy ground-line (sine, ~70 px wavelength, ~12 px amplitude, 3 px stroke, pale) appears in scenes C, B, E at the character feet line - a recurring "ground" glyph.

### 1.4 Depth construction (OBSERVED + INFERRED)
Count of separable layers per scene: about 8-10.
- Scene C: (1) sky + stars, (2) sun with rays, (3) distant palace silhouette, (4) green hills, (5) ground, (6) characters (3 separate cut-outs), (7) glow fireflies, (8) tree canopy + trunk (mid-fore, slightly blurred), (9) foreground out-of-focus green blob bottom-left (heavy blur, ~25 px).

- Blur: depth of field is obvious. In-focus plane = characters. Background (sun, palace) softly blurred (~3-6 px at 923 w); foreground elements (trees, demon in scene B, grass in scene C) strongly blurred (10-25 px). The sun is blurred ~6 px and has bloom.
- Shadows: layer-on-layer soft shadow offset down/right ~6-12 px, blur ~10-16 px, opacity ~35-45% (clearly seen under clouds in scene D, under the slab, and under character limbs).
- Vignette: dark corners in all scenes (strongest in A, B, E) ~35-45% at the edges; scene D has a brown gradient darkening at bottom.

### 1.5 Lighting (OBSERVED)

## 2. Character design

### 2.1 Construction (OBSERVED)
- Paper-doll puppets cut as separate pieces: head+hair, neck, torso, upper arms, forearms/hands (hands are tiny rounded bumps), lower garment (dhoti/skirt/saree), legs, feet. Visible joint overlaps: shoulder discs (Shukra/demon have round pauldron-like shoulder caps with gold ovals), elbows are just bends in a long cylinder arm.
- Arms are separate long capsule shapes hanging at the sides (Kacha, Devayani), or posed (Devayani hands together holding a bowl; Shukra's right arm raised in blessing, left arm hidden; demon arms folded across holding the bowl; demon arms behind the back in scene E).
- Limbs are straight, stiff, tapered rods; legs are two thin capsules with small oval feet. No knees. Minimal walk possible only as slide/bob.
- Faces: tiny, flat, expressionless for the humans (closed almond eyes as two short dark lines, tiny nose line, small mouth, red tilak for Kacha, small ornaments for Devayani). Faces are small (head ~ 1/7 of figure height) relative to long bodies - tall, elongated stylised proportions (roughly 7.5 heads tall).
- The demon: dark purple, angry slanted gold eyes, two white fangs, gold crown; blocky torso with round shoulders; red dhoti with scalloped hem; grey-purple legs.
- Sitting figure (Shukra/Brihaspati): one piece torso+legs in lotus pose, arm separate, beard and bun in white as a separate layer. Seated on a gold cushion on a slab.
- Beveled/embossed shading on each piece (light inner edge top-left) - hence the "cardboard" feel.

### 2.2 How characters are distinguished
- Kacha: bare torso, topknot + red tilak, sacred thread (thin white diagonal line), white dhoti with gold sash and a gold central panel, standing straight.
- Devayani: crimson saree with gold border draped over a yellow blouse, headband/ornament, holding a gold bowl at waist.
- Shukra / Brihaspati (same elder design recoloured): white hair bun, beard, bead mala, cream shawl, raised blessing hand, seated.
- Demon: purple skin, crown, fangs, fiercer eyes. Demon army: tiny horned purple silhouettes with white teardrop spear tips.
- Shapes + colour blocking are strong: each has one saturated garment colour (white, crimson, orange-brown, purple/red).

### 2.3 Poses used
- Frontal standing, arms at sides (Kacha); frontal standing, hands together at waist (Devayani); 3/4-profile seated with a raised arm (guru); frontal stand with arms crossed holding bowl (demon A); frontal with arms behind back (demon E); Kacha as shattered triangular shards (scene B, head piece rotated about 35 degrees, torso piece rotated, separated lower pieces, small shards flying) - a clear "pose" of broken pieces.
- Almost all poses are static, frontal/profile; expression comes from camera move and position slides.

## 6. Why it looks "full and rich" and not flat

1. Many layers (8-10 per scene) with real separation: sky, sun, far hills, structures, mid hills, mid props, characters, foreground trees, foreground blur.
2. Strong depth cues: DOF blur graded by layer (3-6 px background, 15-25 px foreground), atmospheric haze (blue-purple palace silhouette fades into sky), foreground silhouettes darker and bigger.
3. Cast and contact shadows between every layer, with a consistent light direction; bevel highlights on every edge create a thickness illusion.
4. Material texture: paper grain + soft emboss, plus vignette and film grain to unify.
5. Light FX: sun bloom, glow, moonlight cone, rim glow on characters, fireflies and dust; the limited palette with a single gold accent reads cinematic.
6. Constant gentle motion: parallax drift, sun rise/sink, idle bob, sliding character, particles; even a "still" scene never stops moving.
7. Dense detail in set dressing: stitched wave lines, bunting, hundreds of crowd silhouettes, palace line-art, beads, dots on a saddle - detail that is small but numerous.
8. Narrative typography on top (counter, title card, highlighted keyword caption) adds hierarchy.
9. Value structure: each scene has a clear light-to-dark gradient from sky to foreground, with the characters at mid-value contrast against the background and the brightest spot (sun/moon/halo) just beside them.

## 7. Recipe: image model + 2.5D layered renderer (Blender) + ffmpeg captions

### 7.1 Output spec
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 yuv420p, CRF 16-18. Work at 2x layer resolution (2160 px wide layers, parallax needs margin).
- Shot length 3.5-6 s (105-180 frames); cut/dissolve between shots with 8-12 frame crossfade (or 6-frame dip-through-black for the night scene).

### 7.2 Assets to generate per scene (flat images, 1 image = 1 layer, then alpha-cut)
Prompt all in one style tag: "flat vector paper-cut illustration, layered cut paper, no outlines, matte flat colours, limited palette, [palette list], clean edges, isolated on solid magenta background" -> use background removal (rembg / SAM / chroma key) to get RGBA. Generate each layer separately (it keeps shapes clean). Canvas bigger than frame by 15-20% on parallax layers.
Base layer list (reuse across scenes, recolour/tint per scene):
1. L0 sky: gradient + stars (opaque, 1 image, 130% frame).
2. L1 sun or moon (RGBA, separate rays disc), glow added in renderer.
3. L2 far hills/wave band with a faint rim line (RGBA, 130% width).
4. L3 far structures (palace line-art, banner rows, crowd silhouettes) - as one layer, slightly desaturated.
5. L4 mid hills / waves / cloud shelf 1.
6. L5 mid cloud shelf 2 / ground strip with wavy line.
7. L6 characters: each character as a set of parts for rigging (head+hair, torso, upper arm L/R, forearm L/R, lower garment, leg L/R, foot) or at least body + 2 arms + head; shards for shatter scenes.
8. L7 props (bowl, cushion, slab, cow, flags).
9. L8 foreground framing (tree canopy/trunk, dark demon silhouette, grass) - large, 140% width.
10. FX: bokeh/dust png (transparent) 1-2 layers, firefly sprites.
Scene-specific: E - flags + crowd + hill + Shukra; D - 3 cloud shelves + halo rings + dunes; C - tree + palace + sun; B - dark cloud-trees (fg), cow, Kacha shard set; A - 4-5 wave rows, moon, hill.
Character sheet: generate each hero once (Kacha, Devayani, Shukra/Brihaspati recolour, Demon) front-facing, T-pose-like arms down, flat colours, then slice into parts (hand cut or SAM) and keep them as the rig library for all scenes. Keep faces tiny and simple as in the reference (two almond lines, tilak).

### 7.3 Pre-processing per layer (python/OpenCV, before Blender)
- Alpha edge clean: erode 1 px, feather 0.7 px.
- Faux bevel: inner highlight = (alpha - blur(alpha, 2 px)) shifted (-2,-2), white 35-40%, screen; inner shadow shifted (+2,+2), black 30%, multiply; width 2-3 px at 1080 w.
- Paper grain: tile a paper-fibre texture (or generated noise blurred 0.6 px) overlay at 6-8% on every layer (stronger, 10%, on large flats like canopy and waves).
- Slight desaturation + tint per depth: far layers lower contrast and pushed toward the sky hue by 15-25% (atmospheric haze), near layers darker 15-30%.

### 7.4 Blender scene set-up (Eevee Next or Cycles at 32-64 samples; or Python + bpy script generating planes)
- Orthographic or 35-50 mm perspective camera (perspective gives true parallax); layers as image planes with alpha (Alpha Hashed / Blend), spaced in Z. Suggested Z (m, camera at 0, looking -Z... in units of "1 = foreground plane"): sky 40, sun 35, far hills 28, far structures 22, mid hills 16, characters 8, props 7.5, nearer clouds 6, foreground framing 2.5. Scale each plane so it fills the frame at its depth (scale ∝ distance).
- Real shadows: a Sun lamp from upper-left (~35 deg from vertical, rotation about (40, 0, -30)), strength 3, soft size 8-10 deg; planes cast shadows (shadow mode = hashed/opaque from alpha) onto layers behind. Layers must be 0.15-0.4 m apart for the shadow offset to read: target shadow offset 6-12 px (at 1080 w), blur 10-16 px (light angle 5-8 deg), opacity 35-45%. If shadows get too soft/odd, use a compositor approach: per-layer drop shadow node chain (alpha -> offset (+8,+10) -> blur 12 -> multiply 0.4) or bake in pre-processing.
- Fog/haze: mist pass in Eevee world, or per layer mix colour node with distance factor (see 7.3).
- DOF: camera f-stop 2.8-4, focus on the character plane; at 35 mm this gives ~3-6 px blur at Z 25-40 and 12-25 px at Z 2.5. If DOF in eevee is noisy use compositor Defocus node, z-based, or bake per-layer Gaussian blur: sky/sun 5 px, far 3-4 px, mid 0-1, characters 0, foreground 14-22 px (at 1080 w).
- Glow: Bloom (threshold 0.9, intensity 0.15, radius 6) and an additive radial-gradient plane behind the sun/moon (scale 3x sun, opacity 30-50%, a slow +-5% pulse).
- Vignette: compositor ellipse mask blur 0.4, 35-45% darken at corners.
- Film grain: compositor noise 2-4% (applied after everything, luminance-only), plus a faint 1-1.5% flicker.
- Dust/embers: 40-80 particle sprites (3-10 px), drifting upward 20-60 px/s, 40-80% opacity, additive; fireflies: 6-12 gold dots with sine drift 10 px and pulsing glow 2-3 s period.
- Colour grade: per-scene LUT/colour balance (see palettes in 1.1), plus slow grade animation (sunset: lift 0 -> -12% over the shot).

### 7.5 Motion numbers (at 1080x1920; shot 4 s unless noted)
- Camera: dolly-in 4-6% over the shot (scale 1.00 -> 1.05) with ease-in-out, plus lateral truck 40-80 px (3-6% of width), plus optional tilt 20-40 px.
- Parallax speed multipliers relative to the camera truck (1.0 = same speed as the characters): sky 0.05-0.1, sun 0.1 (+ its own rise/sink 70-100 px over 3 s, scale +-10%), far hills 0.2-0.25, far structures 0.35, mid hills 0.5, characters/props 1.0, mid cloud shelves 1.2, foreground framing 1.8-2.4. These follow from planes at different Z with perspective camera.
- Character slide: 250-300 px in 2.5 s with ease-in-out and +-6 px vertical bob at 2 Hz (walk-like), sway +-1.5 deg; seated figures: bob 12-15 px with 4 s period; arm/hand raise 8-10 deg swings at 0.5 Hz.
- Rotation animations (demon rising): pivot at feet, rotate from -90 deg to 0 over 24-36 frames with ease-out and a slight 3-frame overshoot (+4 deg).
- Counter: ffmpeg drawtext or ASS number lines generated per frame; rate ~120-150/s for 0 -> 500 in 3.5-4 s, easing out near the end; label "YEARS OF SERVICE" fades in 8 frames before the digits.
- Shatter: pre-slice the character into 6-10 triangles with a Voronoi/straight-cut in Python; each shard gets an initial velocity (outward 80-250 px/s), angular velocity (+-60-200 deg/s), slight gravity (200-400 px/s2) and fades out over the last 0.5 s; keep the head shard 20-30% slower. Blur and darken the rest of the scene: foreground silhouette 15 px, vignette +15%, saturation -20% during the shatter. Duration 1.5-2.5 s.
- Transitions: 8-12 frame crossfade (or "paper wipe": a foreground cloud shelf slides across, 15 frames); title/chip fade-in 10 frames, hold 1.5-2.5 s, fade-out 10 frames.

### 7.6 Captions with ffmpeg (ASS, burned in)
- Font: Nunito Sans ExtraBold or Mulish ExtraBold (OFL; ship the .ttf and use `fontsdir=`), size ~66-70 px at 1080x1920, centred, vertical position ~ y = 0.65 * 1920 (MarginV ~ 650 px from the bottom, Alignment=2), shadow: `\shad3\blur5\4c&H000000&\4a&H80&` or generate a blurred shadow layer. No outline (Outline=0).
- Colours: default #F4EFE2 (`&HE2EFF4&` in BGR), highlight #D7B35C (`&H5CB3D7&`); use per-keyword override tags `{\c&H5CB3D7&}gods{\c}` chosen in the script (mark keywords by hand or LLM).
- Chunking: 2-3 words per event, 0.7-1.2 s; fade `\fad(120,80)` plus slide-up `\move(540,x,540,y-8,0,150)` (about 8-10 px).
- Titles: Playfair Display Black (OFL) for "KACHA" (~85 px, #4A1A1A, y ~11%) and the counter number (~190 px, #F3EBD3); subtitle Merriweather/Lora SemiBold ~42 px, #4A2C20; "YEARS OF SERVICE" ~38 px with `\fsp14` letter spacing, #D9C79A.
- Example ffmpeg:
  `ffmpeg -i scene.mp4 -vf "ass=captions.ass:fontsdir=fonts,drawtext=fontfile=fonts/PlayfairDisplay-Black.ttf:text='%{eif\:min(500\,trunc((t-12)*130))\:d}':fontsize=190:fontcolor=0xF3EBD3:x=(w-tw)/2:y=210:enable='between(t,12,18)':shadowx=3:shadowy=4:shadowcolor=0x00000066" -c:v libx264 -crf 17 -pix_fmt yuv420p out.mp4`
- Chapter chip: drawbox (thin 2 px gold border `color=0xC9A24A`) + drawtext tracked caps, fade with `alpha='...'` expression.

### 7.7 Quick checklist (what must be in every shot)
- >= 8 layers incl. >= 1 foreground blurred layer and >= 1 haze-tinted far layer.
- Down-right drop shadow 8-10 px / blur 12 px / 40% under each mid/near layer; bevel 2-3 px on every cutout.
- Paper grain 6-8% + film grain 3% + vignette 40%.
- Sun/moon with bloom; at least 20 drifting specks; one warm accent (gold #D4A93C).
- Camera dolly 4-6% + lateral parallax; sun/clouds with their own slow motion; character slide or bob.
- Caption 2-3 words, 1-3 gold keywords per sentence, soft shadow, at 65% height; titles in top 8-17%.
