# GranthStudio DECISION LOG (running record; add an entry EVERY time a decision is taken)

Purpose: so that Adhyay 2, 3 ... reproduce what worked and do not repeat mistakes. `docs/DECISIONS.md` is the binding record (D-xxx / A3 follow-ups); this log is the working journal behind it: what was decided, by whom, why, what went wrong before, and how to repeat it for the next Adhyay. Newest at the bottom. Format: date | decision | who | why / what happened | how to repeat.

## Repeatable recipe for the next Adhyay (kept current; derived from the entries below)
1. Source text: Marathi ovis checked against the printed book by the owner; frozen FINAL answers extracted once with source notes.
2. Story split by Kimi (one call, planner), code-validated, owner edits directly (no extra Kimi call), cross-check advisory only.
3. Owner writes/approves the EASY Marathi + Hindi script per line (one id per line, ovi kept as provenance). Check meaning against the verse.
4. Audio plan: lines, speakers (owner's dialogue table), sound cues from the closed palette.
5. Voices: pick once per recurring character (voice ids stay the same across Adhyayas); generate ALL lines once with `a1_eleven.py` (reuse by key, ledger cap); owner listens; balance loudness in the mix (`--target-dbfs -22`).
6. Measure real clip lengths -> timing with `--no-floor` -> shot budgets = measured need -> new split revision.
7. Bible + image catalog from the frozen research; the owner makes reference sheets first, then one tailored FULL image per shot (no image limit).
8. Kimi directs each scene once with the compact contract (library of presets/sounds/images in the stable prompt prefix, ids-only output, reasoning_effort low).
9. Pilot render of two scenes, then the rest; record every decision here as it is taken.
