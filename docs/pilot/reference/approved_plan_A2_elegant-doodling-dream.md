### Phase 3: audio-first mini test (dual audio + sound effects), before new art
Order: scene plan -> draft Marathi narration (cited, labelled paraphrase where not exact) -> **owner approves** -> Hindi (IndicTrans2 with glossary masking, or another candidate; reviewed by owner; NOT VALIDATED) -> TTS candidates on 10 verses (Indic Parler-TTS Apache-2.0 on Kaggle; IndicF5 needs reference audio + speaker consent; Sarvam/Azure as paid options; owner-read audio as the baseline) -> measure decoded durations -> shot count follows speech (long lines span several 6 s renders, short lines leave silence, never speed speech up; Hindi gets its own timeline on shared pictures) -> manual captions -> 60-90 s bilingual audio storyboard on EXISTING stills (one quote, one teaching beat, one refrain, one long Hindi line).
BILINGUAL TIMING (OWNER DECISION PENDING): ONE video file with switchable Marathi/Hindi audio needs a COMMON picture schedule (reserve enough coverage for BOTH measured utterances at every beat; align picture-event SFX and captions per language); TWO videos (the frozen architecture's 42 releases) can have independent per-language timelines on shared pictures. Do not build the timing code until the owner chooses.
Sound effects (design settled by the repo search + Codex's argument against the outside critique): the DIRECTOR picks sound ids from a CLOSED palette in the same call as the shots (a design choice; no repo does exactly this: MM_StoryAgent, WavJourney, ReelWave, LVAS-Agent and Audio-Oscar all use a separate sound step; MM_StoryAgent's free-text sound agent made sound alignment WORSE (2.53 vs 2.93) and added "excessive" sound; WavJourney only worked because a hand-written compiler places everything (0% vs 56% execution errors); Audio-Oscar fixes speech durations first and derives cue times from them). So: no free-text sound engineering, no second generative sound-director until the owner has heard a first mix.
- `sound_palette.json` (built before Phase 4): about 6 ambience beds, about 15 spot effects, about 3 motifs; each entry = id, file, sha256, licence + source URL (archived), playback defaults (loop, base level). Preference order for sources: owner-recorded foley; retrieval from a licence-checked library (filter each download by licence; CC0 or CC-BY with attribution; nothing NC); generated SFX last (short transients like a door slam lose attack/decay in generators; Stable Audio Open licence only supports sub-$1M-revenue use with conditions).
- Cue schema (director output, no seconds, no dB): `{id, palette_id (closed enum), kind bed|spot|motif, anchor (scene start/end for beds | picture-event | utterance id with before/after), ref (shot or utterance id), label verse-named|STAGING, source_ovi (chapter-qualified, required if verse-named)}`. EVERY cue is anchored symbolically, resolved separately for Marathi and Hindi from the measured audio and the edit; picture-synchronous cues keep picture-event anchoring and, if speech timing conflicts, coverage is adjusted or the cue is blocked for review. A word inside an utterance (the belch) needs an approved pause/event marker or validated alignment; WAV duration alone resolves only utterance boundaries.
- NOT adopted: blanket "cut-point foley", automatic pre-roll, drone swells or preset-triggered sound envelopes (a sound at a cut can imply an unseen action or precede its picture; our real presets are 01 establishing, 02 parallax, 03 push, 04 entrance, 05 bow, 06 blessing, 07 reaction, 08 close-up, 09 dialogue, 10 insert, 11 transition; no pull-back or static preset exists; a 6 s shot does not dictate a sound envelope). Cut-point foley only where an approved pose change supports the event.
- Validator (not the compiler) rejects: unknown palette ids, broken references, unsupported provenance, missing rights evidence, a bed that does not wrap at least one utterance (WavJourney rule), more than one music item at a time, a cap of cues per scene (MM_StoryAgent uses 3 per page), and after resolution any out-of-bounds cue or any SPOT effect overlapping a quote utterance in either language (quiet ambience under a quote stays allowed unless the owner wants complete silence). A sound never implies a miracle or adds a fact.
- A deterministic compiler (plain code, reusing the existing FFmpeg assembly helper; not an LLM, size estimate unproven) schedules, loops, fades and mixes validated cues. Loudness numbers (speech about -15 LUFS, beds about -32 to -35 dB) are listening-test HYPOTHESES from WavJourney's paper/prompt (which disagree with each other), not standards; calibrate on the bilingual mini-test, document measurement windows, final-programme loudness and true-peak ceiling. Ducking is compiler gain reduction, not a director field. WavJourney (CC BY-NC-ND): read its prompt, do not copy its code.

Image-reuse rules go into the Phase 2 contract/prompt BEFORE the bake-off and are FROZEN with it (identical reuse rules, asset inventory and counting method for every candidate; new drawings and adaptation work counted separately; no tuning after seeing candidates, which would contaminate the comparison): a hold over two shots or a crop/camera move on an existing image_id costs zero new art; a new image_id costs owner effort.
