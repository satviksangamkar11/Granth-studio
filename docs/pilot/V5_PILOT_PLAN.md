## Goal
One 60-second pilot from Adhyay 1 in Hindi and Marathi (shared pictures, per-language audio and captions), built with the frozen Builder v2 and the preset library. Original flat-colour look (D-016).

## Inputs needed from the owner before any work
1. The exact Adhyay 1 pilot passage (Marathi and Hindi lines), approved in `docs/pilot/SOURCE_APPROVAL_SHEET.md`: speakers, costume, allowed actions, which lines are narration.
2. The shot list approval (about 6 to 10 shots of 6-10 s, each mapped to a preset) and the images each shot needs (generated by the owner in Flow, same image rules and prep as Phase 1).
3. A backup folder or drive for verified copies.
4. Voice choice by listening test (Indic Parler-TTS, IndicF5, Indic-Speak; all NOT VALIDATED until executed) and licence acceptance on Hugging Face by the owner.

## Steps (each gated by the owner)
1. Approve passage and shot list. (Kimi may draft the shot list from the approved text; cents, ledger-guarded. The open $0.33 reservation must be reconciled first.)
2. Owner makes images; Claude's scripts clean them (sparkle removal, transparency, checker).
3. Voices: ~20 s Hindi and Marathi samples; owner picks; full narration generated; sentence-level timing (word-level only if a Marathi aligner is proven: ctc-forced-aligner / MMS, NOT VALIDATED).
4. Shots: fill presets with the approved images (JSON only), render with the simplest renderer (motion.py / parallax.py / Blender v2); owner reviews contact sheets and watches the clips.
5. Assembly with ffmpeg: shots + narration + captions per language; shorter language trims holds only.
6. Audit: cost, review minutes, consistency; owner approves or rejects.
