## What the frames show (first look, 2026-10-28; a few frames per set, not a full study)
- Flat-vector mythological animation, portrait format. Set 1: an archer in a chariot, then two standing figures at sunset. Sets 2 and 3: the ten-headed Ravana (struggling to escape; arms crushed under the mountain).
- Warm sunset or dusk gradients, layered dark silhouettes (armies, tree line, water, mountain), a foreground wheel or ground strip that frames the shot, a soft light beam, drifting dust and light particles.
- Characters have a thin glowing outline (rim light) and gold costume detail.
- Captions in the lower third: bold white sans-serif in set 1, a serif face with one highlighted word in set 3.
- Sets 2 and 3 carry a large `GetVideoFrames` watermark from the site that extracted the frames; it is NOT part of the animation style.
- Close to our own flat-colour look and to what the presets do (01 establishing layers, 02 parallax, 03 slow push, 08 glow). A closer frame-by-frame study (camera speed, which layers move, how the glow and the beam are made) is still to be done.
